01

Brand idea

Why Halcyon exists and what it will never be.

Halcyon days are the calm stretch of weather in midwinter when the sea goes flat. We roast for that feeling: clear, unhurried, warm.

Promise: a quiet, exact cup, every morning

  • Patient

    Slow drying phases, long rests, no rush to a headline. We wait four days before a coffee leaves the roastery.

    We are patient, never slow

  • Exact

    Every bag prints its roast date, drop temperature and water recipe. Precision is how we show care.

    We are exact, never fussy

  • Sun-warm

    Lisbon light, terracotta roofs, tiled walls. Warmth comes from materials and colour, not from slogans.

    We are warm, never twee

06

Tone of voice

Four attributes, one dial, and the mechanical rules that keep us consistent.

We talk like the person who hands you the cup: warm, specific, never in a hurry to sell.

Calm01

Short sentences, no exclamation marks, space between ideas.

Your coffee is resting. It will be ready on Friday.OMG, hang tight, your amazing coffee is coming!!!

Exact02

Give the number, the unit, the name of the farm.

Washed Caturra, 1,850 m, Finca La Esperanza.Grown high up by passionate farmers.

Warm03

Address one person. Use “you” and “we”. A little Lisbon humour is welcome.

Bom dia. The first pour is on the house if it’s raining.Valued customers are advised that hot beverages are available.

Plain04

Say what it tastes like, not how special it is.

Peach tea, honey, a little bergamot.An artisanal, elevated, curated flavour journey.

NumbersDigits for everything over nine. 15 g, 94 °C, 2:45. Euro before the figure in English (€3.40), after in Portuguese (3,40 €).
Dates and timeDD.MM.YY on packaging. 24-hour clock. “Tuesday” not “Tue”.
Names“Halcyon” on its own after the first mention. Lisbon in English, Lisboa in Portuguese. Never “Halcyon Coffee Co.”
Banned wordsartisanal · curated · elevate · journey · premium · perfect · barista-crafted
CapitalisationSentence case everywhere. Caps only for mono labels of three words or fewer.
PortugueseWritten by a native speaker, not translated. Same rules, same temperature.
