01 — Selected work, 2021–2025

Four projects, one habit: find the moment of doubt and design it out.

M1Fintech2024

Halde: money for the tax bill before it is spent.

Freelancers in Germany owe VAT quarterly, and most learn the number too late. I redesigned Halde's income flow so every invoice quietly fills a tax jar, then tested the language with 22 sole traders until nobody asked "is this mine to spend?"

M2Energy2023

Nordlicht: a heat pump that explains itself.

A municipal utility shipped heat pumps with an app full of flow temperatures. I replaced the dial with one question, "how warm do you want it by 7 am?", and moved the physics into a quiet weekly note. Support calls fell as installs rose.

M3Public infrastructure2022

Pfand: the receipt is the interface.

Bottle-return machines fail loudly: jams, rejected cans, a queue behind you. For a retail group's 310 machines I designed the flow around the receipt, so every rejection tells you what to do and what it cost you, in plain German and in English.

M4Health2025

Kiez Praxis: booking for people with no spare Tuesday.

Neighbourhood clinics lose a fifth of their slots to no-shows. I spent three weeks in two waiting rooms, then designed booking around the shift workers who miss appointments, with a next-free-slot first screen and a one-tap swap instead of a cancel.

02 — About

Born in Lagos, trained in Weimar, working from Neukölln.

I left a bank's design team in 2021 because I wanted to finish things, not hand them over.

Now I work alone, with one or two clients at a time, usually for three to six months. I do research, interaction design and the unglamorous final 10%: error states, empty states, the email nobody reviewed. I write specs engineers actually read, and I will sit in your Slack. I work in English, German and Yoruba, in that order of confidence.
