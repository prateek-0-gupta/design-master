01 — Energy · iOS / Android

Balkonia, plug-in solar

Lead designer, 2025 · 9 months

Two million German balconies can make power, but nobody can tell if it's working. I designed an app that turns watts into something a landlord, a teenager and a grandmother all read the same way.

4.8Store rating
−61%Support tickets
Problem
Inverter data is accurate and useless. Households couldn't tell a cloudy day from a broken panel.
Move
One number, one sentence: "Your balcony covered your fridge today." Everything else lives a layer down.
Outcome
Median set-up fell from 22 to 6 minutes after we replaced the pairing wizard with a camera scan.
02 — Marketplace · Web

Brennofen, kiln time for rent

Founding designer, 2024 · 6 months

Ceramicists share kilns the way cyclists share workshops: by text message and trust. I designed the booking and firing-log system that kept the trust and dropped the spreadsheets.

140Studios in 8 weeks
0Double-booked firings
Problem
A firing is 14 hours and cannot be paused. Calendars built for meetings kept breaking that.
Move
A schedule drawn as a temperature curve. You book the shape of the firing, not a time slot.
Outcome
Hosts adopted it unprompted, then asked for the curve as a printable label for the kiln door.
03 — Fintech · Accessibility

Sparkasse Klar

Senior designer, 2023 · 8 months, with a 12-person team

A banking mode for people over 70 who were being locked out by "simple" apps. We tested with 31 customers, in their kitchens, and built the product around the thing they told us they feared most: pressing the wrong thing.

31Kitchen sessions
3×Remote-banking uptake
Problem
Fear of irreversible actions mattered more than screen size or font size.
Move
Every action is a sentence with a plain "no, call me" exit. Undo is visible, never hidden in a menu.
Outcome
Rolled out to 400 branches; the mode is now the default for new customers over 65.
04 — Travel · Concept to launch

Nachtzug, sleep as a route

Product designer, 2022 · 5 months

Booking a sleeper train meant six tabs and a phone call. I redesigned the planner around the night itself: where you fall asleep, where you wake up, and what the window shows you at 6 a.m.

+38%Sleeper bookings
1Screen to book
Problem
Timetable logic buries the only thing that matters for night travel: how long you can actually sleep.
Move
Results sorted by hours of sleep, with a shaded bar for the night and a small marker for each border crossing.
Outcome
Shortlisted for a German Design Award; the sleep-bar was adopted across the operator's day products.
