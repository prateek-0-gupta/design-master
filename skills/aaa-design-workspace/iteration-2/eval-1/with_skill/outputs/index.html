FAQ

Questions clinics ask first.

Anything else? Talk to a human, usually within the hour.

Is Cadence HIPAA compliant?
Yes. We sign a BAA on every plan, encrypt messages in transit and at rest, and keep message content minimal: first name, time and provider only. Nothing about diagnosis or visit reason is ever sent by SMS.
Do patients need to install an app?
No. It is plain SMS from a local number with your clinic’s name. Patients reply from the messages app they already use.
Which scheduling systems do you work with?
Athena, Elation, Tebra and Google Calendar sync two ways today. Others can be connected through a nightly CSV. Most clinics are live in one afternoon.
What if a patient replies something unexpected?
Cadence only acts on replies it is sure about. Anything else, like “can I bring my daughter?”, lands in a shared inbox flagged for your front desk, and the patient gets a note that a person will reply.
How long until I see fewer no-shows?
Most clinics see a drop in the first two weeks. The median reduction across 1,240 clinics is 41% by day 90. If you do not see a lift after 60 days, we refund the last two months.
Can I cancel anytime?
Monthly plans cancel in one click. Annual plans are refunded pro rata within the first 30 days. You can export every conversation as CSV whenever you like.
