02 — About

Small team, senior hands.

I trained as an architect in Lagos, switched to software in Lisbon, and now work from a studio in Neukölln. Buildings and interfaces share one rule: if people need a sign, the design hasn't finished its job.

I work alone, which means the person you meet in the first call is the person drawing the screens. I join as a product designer for three to six months at a time, sit with your engineers, and leave behind a design system your team can actually maintain.

Before going independent I led design at two Berlin scale-ups, and ran a team of seven at a payments company. I teach a monthly workshop on interface writing at the Hochschule in Weißensee.
