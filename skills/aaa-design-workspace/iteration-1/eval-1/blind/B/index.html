FAQ

Good questions.

Is Cadence HIPAA compliant?

Yes. Messages are encrypted in transit and at rest, we sign a BAA on Clinic and Group plans, and every access is x-logged. Reminders include only the minimum necessary details.

Do patients need to download an app?

No. Everything happens over standard text messages from a local number, so it works on every phone — no logins, no links required.

How long does setup take?

Most clinics are live within a day. Connect your calendar or EHR, import your patient list, pick a reminder template, and Cadence starts texting on the next booking.

Will it work with my existing calendar or EHR?

Cadence syncs with Google Calendar and Outlook out of the box, and integrates with major EHRs on the Group plan. If you use something else, we'll tell you honestly whether we can support it.

What happens if a patient replies with something unexpected?

Cadence handles common intents automatically. Anything it isn't sure about is routed to your front desk's shared inbox with context, so nothing slips through.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no contracts. Cancel from settings and export all of your data whenever you like.
