FAQ

Questions clinic managers ask first.

Anything else? Email hello@cadence.example and a human replies within a business day.

Is Cadence HIPAA compliant?
Cadence is built for HIPAA. We sign a BAA on every plan, encrypt messages in transit and at rest, keep an audit log, and never include diagnosis or provider specialty in a text preview. We are also SOC 2 Type II audited.
What do patients see, and do they need an app?
Just a text from your clinic's own local number. There is no app or login. They reply C, R or X, or write in their own words, and anything the system can't parse goes to your staff inbox.
How fast can we go live?
Most clinics send their first reminders within one working day. We import your upcoming appointments for free, connect your calendar or EHR, and your local number is provisioned in about an hour.
What happens if a patient replies “STOP”?
They are opted out immediately and carrier-compliantly, and flagged on their record so staff know to call instead. Opt-ins and quiet hours are managed automatically.
Are text messages really included?
Yes. Each plan includes a monthly appointment allowance and every reminder, reply and waitlist offer for those appointments is covered. There are no per-message fees.
Can I cancel or switch plans?
Anytime, from settings. Annual plans are refunded pro rata in the first 60 days, and you can export all appointments and message history as CSV.
