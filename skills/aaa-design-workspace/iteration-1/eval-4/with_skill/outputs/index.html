01 · Brand idea

Still water, bright sun

Why we exist and what we promise, in the order a new hire needs it.

We roast light enough to taste the place and dark enough to taste the work. Never louder than the coffee.

Purpose: make a quiet, excellent cup the easiest choice in Lisbon · Promise: roasted < 14 days ago, always

Value 1

Clarity

We say what is in the bag: farm, altitude, roast date. We are clear, never cryptic.

Value 2

Patience

Slow drying, slow roasts, slow mornings. We are unhurried, never idle.

Value 3

Warmth

Sun-coloured, hand-poured, first-name. We are warm, never twee.

05 · Photography

Window light, no theatre

Real people, real cafés, real mornings. Shot where we work.

Photograph the quiet half-second before the first sip. One subject, one light, no staging.

Frames below are art-direction sketches, not final photography

35 mm, f/2–f/2.8

Eye level or 30° down

We shoot

  • Ceramic cups and paper cups in use, with fingerprints and rings
  • Lisbon light: tiles, tram wires, morning haze off the Tagus
  • Our team at work, named in the caption

We never shoot

  • Steam added in post, or beans spilling out of a heart
  • Stock hands, laptops-at-a-café, flat-lay props
  • Heavy filters, HDR, or crushed pure-black shadows

06 · Tone of voice

Say it like a friend who roasts

Four attributes, a dial for context, and the mechanical rules.

Plain words, exact numbers, one small kindness per message.

Languages: pt-PT first, English second · both are written, never translated word for word

Clear

Lead with the fact. Numbers over adjectives.

We sayRoasted 3 Oct. Best before 24 Oct.

We don't sayFreshly roasted for peak flavour!

Warm

Use first names and short sentences. Thank people once.

We sayObrigado, Marta. Your usual is ready.

We don't sayDear valued customer.

Unhurried

No urgency tricks. No countdowns. No exclamation marks in a row.

We sayThe Guji lot is here until it's gone.

We don't sayHURRY! Only 3 bags left!!!

Curious

Share what we're tasting, then let the cup speak.

We sayPeach, jasmine, a little black tea.

We don't sayAn artisanal, ethically-sourced journey.

Tone dial · same voice, three rooms

Filter. Today it's Guji, washed. Peach and jasmine.

Mechanical rules

Currency€3,20 in Portuguese, €3.20 in English. Euro sign always before the figure.
Time & date24-hour clock: 07:30–17:00. Dates as 3 Oct, never 10/3.
Weights250 g with a space. Altitude 1,950 m.
Banned wordsartisanal, curated, journey, perfect, "elevate", "your daily fix", anything with !!
NamingHalcyon on first mention, "we" after. Never "HCR". Blends in title case (Bom Dia), origins by farm.

08 · Motion

One slow sunrise

The only brand movement is the mark rising. Everything else is a quick fade.

Rise slowly, leave quickly. Never loop.

Rise: 900 ms · cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1) · Fade: 200 ms · Reduced motion: opacity only

Use the rise once per screen, on load or when a confirmation lands. Hold for 600 ms, then fade out in 200 ms.

State: resting. The button gives feedback in under 100 ms.
