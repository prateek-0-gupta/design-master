Brand Identity Guidelines · v1.0 · Lisbon

Calm waters, careful roasts.

Halcyon Coffee Roasters is a small specialty roaster on the hills above the Tagus. This guide keeps everything we make as unhurried and clear as a good morning light.

01 — Logo

The Kingfisher Seed

The halcyon is the mythical kingfisher that calms the sea. Our mark joins a coffee seed (the bird’s dive) with two Atlantic waves, held in a circle like a Lisbon azulejo roundel.

Halcyon
COFFEE ROASTERS

Halcyon
Stacked lockup — Cream on Espresso
Reversed on Azulejo
Reversed on Terracotta
One-colour black

Do

  • Keep clear space equal to the seed’s height (¼ of the mark) on all sides.
  • Use the mark alone when it is smaller than 24 mm / 80 px wide lockup is too small.
  • Minimum size: 16 mm print, 24 px screen for the mark; 32 mm for the full lockup.
  • Use Azulejo, Cream, Espresso or white only, with strong contrast.

Don’t

  • Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows or gradients.
  • Recolour in off-palette colours, or place on busy photography without a solid panel.
  • Separate the waves from the seed or redraw the wordmark in another typeface.
  • Use Terracotta on Azulejo — the contrast is too low.
Incorrect: stretched · rotated and off-palette · low contrast on busy background
02 — Colour

Tile, terracotta & crema

Drawn from the city: glazed blue tiles, terracotta rooftops, whitewashed walls and a good espresso. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Proportion

Crema 40 · Azulejo 25 · Espresso 15 · Telha 10 · supports 10. Telha is a spark, never a field.

Accessible pairs

Espresso on Crema 14.6:1 · Crema on Azulejo 7.5:1 · Crema on Espresso 14.6:1 · Telha text only at 24px+ on Crema (3.2:1).

04 — Photography

Morning light, honest hands

Natural window light, slightly low and from the side. Real people, real moments, nothing staged. Warm shadows, lifted blacks, no heavy filters.

Still life — a cup in side light, steam visible
Macro — beans, texture, shallow depth
City — Lisbon rooftops, tiles and the river
People — roaster at work, face soft, hands sharp

Look for

  • Side or window light, warm whites
  • Hands, steam, grinds, tactile detail
  • Tile, stone, linen, wood and brass surfaces
  • Space around the subject for type

Avoid

  • Flash, harsh overhead light, heavy vignettes
  • Stock-style smiling baristas
  • Latte art cliches, cold blue grading
  • Cluttered tabletops and visible competitor logos
05 — Tone of voice

Warm, plain-spoken, a little salty

We talk like the person behind the counter who knows the farmer’s name — never like a catalogue. Short sentences. Specific over superlative. Humour is dry and sea-breezy.

An unparalleled, premium coffee experience crafted for connoisseurs.

Our ethically sourced beans are best-in-class.
Ana Lucía grows this one at 1,800 metres. We pay her twice the market price.
Hurry! Limited stock, order now!!
Only forty bags this time. No rush, but no promises.

Write in sentence case. Use Portuguese for local touchpoints (“Bom dia”) and keep English for tasting notes. No exclamation marks, no “artisanal”.
