                        Here’s the thing:
                        Not everyone’s
                        going to get it.
                        Staring at a screen was fine for a while, but canned
                        content had its day. We’re building something
                        better, and we’re not alone.
                        From everyone in chat, to the streamers making
                        sure the show goes on. We’re a collective cast of
                        millions going all-in to make moments together,
                        directing the action as we watch it.
                        We don’t always know what’s next, and we like it
                        that way. Because multiplayer is everything—our
                        how and our why. It’s powered by all of us. And for
                        some reason, by Bob Ross too.
                        This started as a place to stream. Together, we’re
                        making it Twitch.

be left for special instances.
