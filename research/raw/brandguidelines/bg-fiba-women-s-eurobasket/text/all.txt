1. BRAND CONCEPT
BRAND CONCEPT


The idea

The game of basketball is fast,
explosive and thrilling. Women’s
basketball, especially so. Let the
game be portrayed and perceived
as the most exciting game there is.
The brand identity with a
recognisable and daring graphical
palette needs to combine the game
and the lifestyle and highlight the
hosts that will set the stage for the
sporting event of the year.

OFFICIAL Logo


Overview

This is the official logo for the
FIBA Women’s EuroBasket 2023.
The FIBA Women’s EuroBasket
2023 logo is comprised of three
primary elements:
• Symbol
• Permanent Competition Mark
• Host Countries                    SYMBOL
The symbol reflects basketball,
Molten ball inner seames, Fans,
the Trophy, the Mediterranean
sea, the Alps and the year of the
tournament.

OFFICIAL Logo


Clear Area

To ensure the FIBA Women’s
EuroBasket 2023 logo has
consistent, optimal legibility and
prominence, an area of clear space
should be maintained around it.
The height of the word
‘EUROBASKET’ is the minimum
exclusion area that has been created
around the logo to ensure visibility
and distinctiveness.
The dimensions of the exclusion area
apply to the portrait and landscape
versions of the official logo for any
kind of background.

Graphic Elements

Composite cluster
Graphic clusters as generic
basketball applications are used
through branding applications to
step up the excitement and bring
colors to big or long layouts.
The elements in the cluster grow in
the gradient manner from the outer
edge to the center of the cluster.
This way we avoid sharp border
where the cluster ends. It gives the
feel of a blur.
We compose and use the cluster         Composite cluster / portrait
within the format considering the
edges of the individual elements.
