Partner lockups
To make sure the brand lives consistently
with another Partner brand, it must respect
this spacing.

The Make logo and the Partner logo should
always be divided by a line and both logos
should feel of equal size.                    x   x   x
The Partner lockup should be used for
communication about approved Make
partnerships.

Don’t use Make name or symbols as part of
your own.

Don’t use Make in advertising without our
express approval.
