NorthAlley             Introduction                            04




Introduction
Our Brand is much more than a logo. It is a collection of
thoughts, ideas, visuals; it is a summation of all that we,
as a company, do and say.


This is a rundown of all the basic elements that make up
our brand.Stick to these guidelines when designing or
creating visual or written content for the brand and it will
ease understanding for brand teams and managers.These
guidelines will help deliver consistency in everything we
do – from ads to emails, merchandise to events. So that
everyone who meets us, will understand what NorthAlley
is all about.


Say hello to the most powerful thing we have: our Brand.

The Brand
Welcome to NorthAlley, where technology meets

commitment to standing out in the digital revolution.
Join us on this transformative journey, where
NorthAlley is not just a tech company but a beacon for
innovative success.

The Logo
Our logo is central to our brand. It's bold, unique, and
captures the essence of NorthAlley. It features a
compass, symbolizing our role as a reliable guide in the
tech industry. Like a compass points north, we aim to
provide direction and clarity for our clients.


Use it consistently across all communications following
the guidelines provided for maximum impact.

NorthAlley            Co-branding                        11




Co-branding                                                   x   0.16x




During co-branding or logo lockup arrangement where
your logo is combined or associated with another
business or partner's logo for collaborative purposes,
we must ensure a cohesive and professional
appearance, maintaining a balanced visual hierarchy.


We separate our logo and the partners logo by 0.16x.
Our partners logo should not exceed the size height of
our logo, except some overhanging elements in logos
where it’s unavoidable; For example the Taj logo here.
