# Twitch Identity Guidelines (Speedrun Edition) — Collins

## 1. Snapshot
- **Subject:** A 19-page landscape deck (1224 × 792 pt, rendered at 1400 × 906 px) for the 2020 Twitch identity, dated "V 2.0, 01.07.21". It covers logos, lockups, colour, typography, layout and voice, with environmental mock-ups in between.
- **Why it's remarkable:** The system's signature is a pixel-blocky wordmark whose 3D *extrusion* becomes a canvas for gradients. On posters the extrusion stretches into a long rainbow beam (orange→purple, green→cyan→purple) between two mirrored wordmarks. A logo becomes a graphic device without new artwork.

## 2. Composition & layout
- **Spec pages** (5, 6, 8, 11, 12, 15, 18) share one template. A left text rail runs from x≈41 px to ≈300 px of 1400 (about 21% of the width). It holds a 30 px purple section title and 12–13 px body copy. Content cards run from x≈375 px to 1358 px and are filled Ice #F0F0FF on white, with a corner radius of about 18 px (≈16 pt) and gutters of about 17 px. The footer "Twitch Identity Guidelines" (≈8 px) sits at y≈881, with the folio at the right.
- Card labels follow a "Wordmark →" idiom: small black text with an arrow, top-left inside the card at a 24 px inset. On p5 the grid is 2:1 (large logo card plus narrow clearspace card) × 3 rows.
- **Full-bleed pages** (3, 4, 7, 10, 13, 14, 16, 17) are photographic mock-ups with no copy: phone splash, desktop Browse UI, a cube sign with the Glitch mark, wheat-paste posters and billboards. They act as chapter breathers.
- Cover (p1) and back cover (p19) are solid purple #9147FF at 98.8% and 96.7% coverage, with a tiny white wordmark top-left and edition text.

## 3. Typography
- **Embedded fonts (pdffonts):** Roobert Light, Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold, Heavy and MediumItalic. Roobert is the only typeface: Displaay's geometric sans with a large x-height and flat terminals. The deck calls it "Twitch Roobert".
- **Specs as size/leading/tracking triplets (p12):**
  - Headline: Roobert SemiBold 80/80/−20, i.e. leading 1.0 and tracking −0.02 em.
  - Highlight/Call-out: Roobert Large 30/33/−15.
  - Body: Roobert Medium 16/20/−10.
  - Body Small: Roobert Medium 11/14/0.
- **Weight usage:** there are six weights. Regular, Medium, Bold and SemiBold are the common ones. Light and Heavy are kept "for special instances". Italics are allowed "(tastefully)" for emphasis.
- **Layout rule (p15):** no more than 3 type styles per piece. Six approved poster compositions are shown, all in Ice with purple type: headline top-left, centred top, left-aligned paragraph, centred middle, split top/bottom, and the "Streaming Effect: Crop & Repetition". The last one stacks the same word five times, cropped at the top and bottom edges. It reappears on billboards (p13 "Poki", p14 "Myth").
- The wordmark is custom lettering, not set type. It is chunky, stencil-pixel and reminiscent of the old logo.

## 5. Depth & material
- The page UI is flat: no shadows, only rounded Ice cards on white or black.
- Depth lives entirely in the logo. The Extruded Wordmark has a lower-right offset solid in purple, outlined in white or black (p5, p11). On posters the extrusion is pulled into a long perspective trail filled with a vertical multi-stop gradient from the Accent palette (p10, p14, p16), on a 3D-rendered Glitch cube sign (p7).

## 6. Components & patterns
- **Three logo forms (p5).** Wordmark (flat; internal, owned channels, partnerships), Extruded Wordmark ("primary", outbound and expressive), and Glitch (the speech-bubble mascot/monogram).
- **Logo pairing matrix (p11).** 3 forms × 3 grounds (Ice, Purple, Black). On purple the flat wordmark may be white or black, and the extruded wordmark becomes white with a black extrusion.
- **Lockups (p6).** Internal lockup "twitch developers": the descriptor is set in light Roobert, its x-height aligns to the wordmark's x-height, and a gap of x separates them. Partnership lockup "twitch · FORTNITE": cap heights aligned, a centre line, and spacing of 2x | x dot | 2x.
- **Voice matrix (p18).** Four voice pillars (Mischievous, Purposeful, Iterative, Revolutionary), each with a one-line tagline. Two tonal scales: Functional (Casual / Neutral / Firm) and Emotional (Reassuring / Encouraging / Celebratory), each with "Ex." use cases in grey.

## 7. Motion
None is documented. The "Streaming Effect: Crop & Repetition" and the gradient extrusion are static, but they suggest motion (scrolling or stuttering repetition). The doc gives no durations or easing.

## 8. Brand system
**Logo rules**
- **Clearspace (p5)** is drawn as a grid with an "x" module in each corner cell. The module looks like the height of the wordmark's ascender notch (the top of the "t"/"i"). For Glitch, a "1/2" annotation suggests half the module applies on the compact side.
- **Minimum size is never stated.** There are also no misuse ("don't") examples anywhere in the 19 pages, which is the biggest gap for a logo whose extrusion is easy to distort.
- **Usage hierarchy** is unusually explicit. The flat wordmark is "representational" and internal. The extruded wordmark is the primary, outbound one. Glitch is "the quickest way of saying Twitch without spelling it out".
- **Colour pairings:** the text says "only four color pairings" but lists three (Purple on Ice, Black Ops on Purple, Purple on Black). The grid on p11 also shows white-on-purple. This is a copy inconsistency.

**Voice & tone**
- The voice targets "the friend who plays games with you".
- The opening manifesto (p2, "Here's the thing: Not everyone's going to get it.") demonstrates that voice instead of describing it.
- The two-axis tonal scale on p18 is the most reusable artefact in the deck: three functional registers times three emotional registers, each tied to concrete channels.

**Imagery**
- There is no imagery chapter. Creator photography appears only in mock-ups (p2 collage, p13/14/17 billboards): cut-out creators on black or purple panels, with the creator's name in giant cropped Roobert behind them.
- The back cover notes that photos are illustrative and need permissions.

**Token decisions worth stealing**
- A three-colour core (brand, black, a tinted near-white `#F0F0FF` instead of pure white) with a 24-colour expressive set fenced off for "turbo mode".
- Type specs written as `size/leading/tracking` triplets.
- Tone as a 3×2 matrix with example channels.

## 9. UX
- The deck is fast to scan. Every spec page has the same rail-plus-cards layout, and card labels with "→" read like UI.
- For a working designer it is thin. There are no minimum sizes, no misuse page, no grid specs beyond poster thumbnails, no imagery or iconography rules, and no digital/UI tokens.
- The secondary palette has 24 chips but no guidance on which gradients are allowed. The posters use orange→purple and green→cyan→purple, but no recipe is given.
- "Speedrun Edition" is honest: this is the summary deck, not the full toolkit.

## 10. Craft signals
- The Widow swatch (#BFABFF) lists HSB "264.32 / 72.55 / 100", copied from Twitch Purple. Its real values are about 254° / 33% / 100%. This is a copy-paste error in the spec table.
- The published Purple #9146FF and the rendered #9147FF differ by one unit (a colour-management artefact, harmless).
- The lockups use red hairline guides labelled "Cap Height", "X-Height" and "Center". This is the clearest part of the logo spec.
- Card corner radius (~18 px), inset (~24 px) and gutter (~17 px) are consistent across pp5, 6, 11, 12, 15 and 18.
- Swatch chips on p9 use about 12 px radii and set their text in a contrasting tone per chip. On the pale chips, grey text on Ice is near-illegible (Mega, Legend and Jiggle labels are washed out).
- p11 claims four pairings but shows three in the text.

## 12. Rating
| Axis | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Aesthetics | 8 | Confident three-colour core with a gradient-extrusion device that makes the posters sing. The spec pages are tidy rounded-card layouts. |
| Originality | 7 | The gradient extrusion beam and the "crop & repetition" streaming effect are fresh. The rest is a familiar tech-brand deck. |
| Usability | 6 | Lockup geometry, type triplets and the tone matrix are directly usable. There is no minimum size, no misuse page, no imagery or gradient rules, and the doc's own purple-on-Ice body text fails AA. |
| Craft | 6 | Consistent template, but with errors: "four pairings" lists three, and the Widow HSB values are copy-pasted. |
