# BellaNova Clinic Brand Guidelines — Dhaval Modi

## 1. Snapshot
- **Subject:** a 53-page, 16:9 (1024×576 pt) identity deck for BellaNova, a dermatology and aesthetic clinic (the addresses point to Oman). It covers strategy and personality, colour, an English and Arabic logo, a star icon, type, photography, patterns and 11 application mock-ups.
- **Why it's remarkable:** a calm luxury-beauty system. Deep indigo, pale lavender and a skin-tone sienna are justified as representing "the diverse spectrum of skin tones". A single four-point "sparkle" is unpacked into a six-star icon, a broken-line lattice pattern and a dot grid.

## 2. Composition & layout
- **Page types:**
  1. **Part dividers** (p4, p8, p13): indigo #120A45 with a large lavender serif title bottom-left, a "01/02/03" numeral, and the line pattern bleeding off the right edge.
  2. **Chapter dividers** (p15, p21, p28, p32, p36, p40): lavender #EBD4F7 with an indigo title.
  3. **Content pages:** white or light grey #efefef. A breadcrumb running head sits at the top-left (e.g. "BELLANOVA BRAND GUIDELINES / HOW IT LOOKS / COLOUR PALETTE") in about 10 px tracked caps at y ≈ 62 px on the 1400 px render. The folio is top-right.
  4. **Statement pages** (p5, p9, p14, p41): a half-bleed portrait on the left (0–245 px of the 485 px thumbnail, about 50%) and a centred serif statement on the right.
- **Margins:** the left margin is about 55 px. The text column is about 55–285 px (about 16% of width); figures start at about 385–470 px.
- **Image-led pages:** p2, p7 and p11 are full-bleed texture photos (bubbles, skin, water) with centred serif headlines.

## 3. Typography
- **Fonts:** pdffonts lists 54 unnamed Type 3 fonts, so the names come from the document. Headlines and body are **Messina Serif**; subheads and labels are **Noir Pro** in all caps; **Araboto** is the Arabic face (p29–30).
- **Hierarchy spec (p31), the most useful page:**
  - Messina Serif display: leading 1.1×, tracking 0.
  - Noir Pro caps title: leading 1.1×, tracking "+90" (about 0.09 em, written as "+90 EM").
  - Messina Serif body: leading 1.4×, tracking 0.
  - Sizes are relative: Noir Pro is equal to or larger than body; Messina display is larger than body. No absolute sizes or scale ratio are given.
- **Measured on the 1400 px renders:** display "Modern care" about 80 px; statement headlines about 40 px; body about 15 px at roughly 1.4 leading; Noir Pro section labels about 18 px caps with very wide tracking; running head about 10 px.
- **Copy error:** the Arabic type page says the face reflects the brand's commitment to "the gaming community". That text was pasted from another project.

- **Shares:** measured from the proportion donut on p20, by angle. The sampled p017 page gives lavender 33.8%, indigo 28.8%, sienna 20.7%.
- **Spec error:** Grey 200 is labelled **#C08F72** (the sienna hex), while its RGB reads 223/223/225 (= #DFDFE1).
- **Tints:** the primary page also shows 30% and 10% tint chips for lavender and sienna.
- **Gradient (p19):** Pale Lavender at 100% fading to 0% opacity at 45°, from bottom-left to top-right.

WCAG:
- #120A45 on #EBD4F7: 13.24:1.
- #120A45 on white: 18.18:1.
- #120A45 on #C08F72: 6.42:1.
- **#C08F72 on white: 2.83:1 (fail).** The website mock (p51) sets a lead paragraph in sienna on white.
- #EBD4F7 on white: 1.37:1, so the lavender tiles on white need a container edge.

## 5. Depth & material
- **Flat graphics:** depth comes from photography of macro skin, petals, water caustics and bubbles. These read as "skin, water, glow" textures.
- **Mock-ups:** soft-shadow mock-ups on #efefef for stationery, the bag and pens.
- **Shape language:** card and screen radii of about 16 px (website mock, ID cards). There are no other effects.

## 6. Components & patterns
- **Icon (p22):** six four-point stars arranged in a ring, read as "Beautiful Star" or "Beautiful Flower".
- **Wordmark:** high-contrast display caps with a stylised "LL" (the second L raised). "CLINIC" sits below in widely tracked Noir Pro.
- **Logo variations (p25):** centred, left-aligned and horizontal. A circular "Authentically Beautiful" seal (p26).
- **Patterns (p38):**
  - **Print:** a lattice of broken curved lines in sienna or lavender, built from star halves.
  - **Digital:** a 5×5 grid of indigo four-point stars, used in the web mock as a faint lavender dot field.
  - **Position & scale (p39):** crop freely, keep the line weight readable.
- **Applications (p41–51):** business cards (white, lavender, sienna variants), letterhead, envelope, ID card, notepad, file folder, bag, uniform, pen, and a website hero with "Book appointment →" text links.

## 8. Brand system
**Strategy and voice (p5–12).**
- **Mission:** personalised, evidence-based skincare.
- **Vision:** a trusted leader in dermatology.
- **Purpose:** "Beyond brightening."
- **Brand essence:** two values, Beautiful and Modern, each one line.
- **Tone of voice:** "Beyond expectations", one paragraph with no do/don't examples.
- **Brand emotions:** Modern, Optimistic, Fresh, Inclusive, Authentic.
- **House style:** short serif statements ending in a full stop ("Tailored modern care, just for you.").

**Logo rules (p22–27).**
- **Clear space:** x = the square cell occupied by the icon, applied on all sides.
- **Minimum height:** **80 px screen / 8 mm print**, measured from the cap top to the base of "CLINIC".
- **Alignment:** when centred, centre on the "BellaNova" letters only and leave the icon out of the measurement. That is a sensible optical rule, poorly written ("It's not nessesory…").
- **Missing:** no misuse page, no colour-reversal matrix and no minimum size for the icon alone. The Arabic logo (p24) gets one paragraph.

**Colour usage.** The p20 donut is the operative rule: lavender and indigo dominate, sienna supports, and secondaries are slivers. The secondary palette is to be used "judiciously, only when essential".

**Photography (p33–35).** Three categories:
- **People:** diverse, soft and well-lit portraits on plain or natural backgrounds.
- **Treatment:** show procedures without "intimidating" tools.
- **Clinic:** bright, white interiors.

A disclaimer (p52) says the photos are not owned by BellaNova and may not be reused.

**Document structure (TOC p3):**
1. Cover, p1; intro "Beauty within you", p2; contents, p3
2. 01 The Brand, p4–7: brand story, strategy, purpose
3. 02 Brand Personality, p8–12: overview, brand essence, tone of voice, brand emotions
4. 03 How It Looks, p13–51:
   - Our reflection, p14
   - Colour Palette, p15–20
   - Our Logo, p21–27
   - Our Typography, p28–31
   - Our Photography, p32–35
   - Our Patterns, p36–39
   - Brand Applications, p40–51
5. Image disclaimer, p52
6. Thank-you back cover, p53

The chapter dividers number Colour 01, Logo 02, Typography 03, Photography **03** (duplicate), Patterns 04, Applications 05.

**Tokens worth stealing:** leading 1.1× for display and 1.4× for body; caps labels at +90 tracking; a lavender gradient at 45° to 0% opacity as an atmospheric wash; a colour-wheel proportion graphic in place of a vague "use mostly X".

## 9. UX
It is a pleasure to browse, and the breadcrumb header makes orientation easy. A designer would struggle in practice, though:
- no absolute type sizes;
- no logo misuse or reversal rules;
- one wrong hex;
- pasted copy that is factually wrong;
- the only accent recommended for web text (sienna) fails contrast on white.

The applications are attractive but carry no specifications: no sizes, stocks or margins.

## 10. Craft signals
- Leading spec 1.1× (Messina display, Noir caps) versus 1.4× (Messina body), with tracking 0 and +90 (p31).
- Minimum logo height 80 px / 8 mm, and the clear-space cell equals the icon's bounding square (p27).
- Gradient: #EBD4F7 at 45°, 100% → 0% opacity (p19).
- Colour donut by angle: lavender ≈134°, indigo ≈105°, sienna ≈45°, five secondaries ≈15° each (p20).
- Two pattern densities: a large lattice for print and a star dot-grid for digital (p38).
- Breadcrumb running head with folio on every content page.

## 12. Rating
| Axis | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Aesthetics | 8 | A cohesive indigo, lavender and sienna triad with a refined serif and plenty of whitespace. The photography is curated to the palette. |
| Originality | 6 | The star-to-pattern derivation is nice, but the whole thing follows the familiar boutique-beauty template. |
| Usability | 5 | Good leading and tracking rules and a proportion wheel. No misuse rules, no absolute sizes, and no application specs. |
| Craft | 5 | Polished layouts undercut by a wrong hex, copy pasted from another project, a duplicated section number, typos, and a failing text colour. |
