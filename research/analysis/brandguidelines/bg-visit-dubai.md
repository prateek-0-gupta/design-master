# Dubai Brand Guidelines 2023 — Visit Dubai (DET in-house)

## 1. Snapshot
- **Subject:** a 51-page, 16:9 deck (exported at 3628×2041 pt) for the destination brand "Dubai". It covers the brand foundation, four brandmarks (Government, Economy & Tourism, City, Calendar, Business Events), type, three colour palettes, usage rules, applications, icons, social, visual content, tone of voice, events, merchandise, partnerships and terms of use.
- **Why it's remarkable:** the wordmark fuses two scripts. The Latin "DUBAI" and the Arabic دبي share strokes, with the Arabic letterforms standing in for the U and B (p9). The PDF also works as a hub: every section ends in a pill-shaped download button that links out to the Bynder DAM.

## 2. Composition & layout
- **Template:** two kinds of page.
  - **Section dividers** (13 of them, e.g. p4, p7, p13, p15, p19, p23, p33, p35, p37, p39, p41, p44, p46, p49) are solid Dubai Blue (sampled #009bde, 79% of p3) with one white uppercase DIN title set left at about 40% height.
  - **Content pages** are white. They carry a home icon in a 40 px circle at top-left, "DUBAI BRAND GUIDELINES 2023" centred in about 10 px grey caps, a faded #e0e0e0 brandmark at top-right, the blue title at x ≈ 110 px and y ≈ 130 px (1400 px render), and a blue folio at bottom-right.
- **Columns:** content pages split into a text column of about 110–470 px and a figure field of about 500–1290 px, sometimes divided by a 1 px grey vertical rule (p5, p9, p16).
- **Section close:** a blue download pill sits bottom-left (about 210×60 px, corner radius about 24 px on the top-right and bottom-left only, echoing the brand curves).
- **Density:** low. Many pages hold one diagram. The cover is a full-bleed photo of the Museum of the Future with a frosted, blurred title panel, about 555×230 px.

## 3. Typography
- **Embedded (pdffonts):** DINPro Light/Regular/Medium/Bold, DIN Light/Regular/Medium/Bold and DINNextLTArabic Regular/Bold. The document is set entirely in DIN: titles in DIN Light caps at about 28 px on the 1400 px render (about 2% of page width), body in DIN Light at about 17 px, labels in DIN Medium caps at about 13 px.
- **Spec (p14), split by channel:**
  - Brandmark supporting text: Dubai BT.
  - Print: DIN Next LT (Arabic and Cyrillic), DIN (English), PingFang SC (Mandarin), and Dubai Font as a secondary face "for emails, official letters, and presentations ONLY".
  - Digital: Droid Kufic (Arabic), Calibri (English), and Experiment Brush Script for calligraphy "for social media usage ONLY".
- **Assessment:** English web copy in Calibri while print uses DIN breaks the identity across channels. No sizes, weights, leading or hierarchy are specified anywhere; the typography section is a font-download list.

## 5. Depth & material
- The pages themselves are flat.
- **Depth comes from photography:** dusk skylines, desert and Burj Al Arab (p26, p31, p38).
- **Mock-ups:** soft-shadow mock-ups of magazines, roll-ups and bags (p27–30, p32).
- **Cover:** the only glass effect, a translucent blurred title panel.
- **Text boxes on photos:** must keep at least 70% fill opacity (p26).

## 6. Components & patterns
- **Brand shapes** (p25): three outline containers "extracted from the brandmark curves". Each is a rectangle with one or two large quarter-round corners, plus a teardrop with a single sharp corner. They frame photos (rounded top-right) and carry the "Discover all that's possible in Dubai." CTA box and the "CLICK HERE" circle.
- **Download pills:** blue, purple, red or orange depending on sub-brand (p11 uses red and orange for Calendar).
- **Useful-links grid** (p3): 9 tiles of about 140×120 px, each with an icon and a two-line caps label (BRAND **LOGOS**, Regular + Bold). Each tile opens the DAM.
- **Icon set** (p34): about 40 outline icons in Contact / Travel & Tourism / Attractions groups. The only drawing rule is that each icon has "a gap that ends with a curve".
- **Navigation:** 101 link annotations in the PDF, 65 internal (home and contents) and 26 unique external links to dubaitourism.getbynder.com.

## 7. Motion
None in the PDF. "End card animation" and "brand destination videos" are linked out (p3, p38) but not specified here.

## 8. Brand system
**Brandmarks (p7–12).** The architecture is an umbrella. The Government of Dubai calligraphic mark sits apart, and four "DUBAI" marks share one form:
- Department of Economy and Tourism (with bilingual descriptor);
- City of Dubai;
- Dubai Calendar;
- Dubai Business Events.

Each comes in white (dark backgrounds), original colour (only on an all-white or all-black design without an image) and black (light backgrounds). Sub-brands change the colour pair: City is blue + purple, Calendar is red + yellow, Business Events is blue + teal. The anatomy page shows the two layers, English and Arabic, but gives no construction grid, stroke values or angle data.

**Placement (p20–21, p24):**
- **Exclusion zone:** "1 daal". The module is the head of the letter A, which doubles as the Arabic د, applied at all four corners. A clear-space unit taken from a letter shared by both scripts is the cleverest rule in the document.
- **Alignment:** align to the Latin baseline, not to the Arabic dots that hang below it.
- **Minimum size:** 20 mm wide, all brandmarks.
- **Position:** top or bottom right corner, or top or bottom centre.
- **Size:** **¼ of page width (portrait) or ⅙ (landscape).**

**Misuse (p22):** eight crossed-out examples: letter spacing, proportions, effects or drop shadows, unapproved colours, blur, cropping, removing elements, italicising.

**Usage on imagery (p26):** white logo only on photos; text box at 70% opacity or more; box sits inside the artwork, never on its edge. Emailers have a fixed width of 640 px and a minimum height of 480 px (p29). Roll-ups come in three options: full image, image with shapes, or full colour (p30).

**Voice (p5–6, p40).**
- **Strategic framework:** a vision of being the #1 "most visited" city; a proposition of "Dubai, where dynamic contrasts meet endless possibilities"; four personality traits (Welcoming, Inspiring, Spirited, Authentic); and a promise "to always be remarkable".
- **Manifesto:** a short first-person poem from the city ("I am where cultures fuse…").
- **Tone of voice:** a set of four gradient sliders, Volume, Energy, Sociability and Attitude. Each has five labelled stops and a "THIS IS US" arrow, for example between Conversational and Effusive on Volume, and between Modest and Confident on Attitude. That is a precise, reusable way to pin down tone.

**Imagery (p38):** every shot must carry at least one of three keys, people, places or detail. A story can combine all three from one shoot.

**Partnerships (p47–48):** endorsement criteria. The lockup spacing is "Minimum 5x, depending on space", and the Dubai mark sits top or bottom right on partner collateral.

**Document structure (p3 contents):**
1. Cover and preamble, p1–2
2. Contents and useful links, p3
3. Brand Foundation, p4–6 (strategic framework, manifesto)
4. Brandmarks, p7–12
5. Typography, p13–14
6. Colour Palettes, p15–18
7. Brand Usage, p19–22
8. Applications, p23–32 (position and size, shapes, brandmark usage, print ads, booklets, emailer, roll-ups, digital ads, black brandmark)
9. Iconography, p33–34
10. Social Media, p35–36
11. Visual Content, p37–38
12. Tone of Voice, p39–40
13. Events, p41–43 (exhibitions, interiors)
14. Merchandise, p44–45
15. Partnerships, p46–48
16. Terms of Use, p49–50
17. Closing logos, p51

**Worth stealing:** a clear-space unit built from a glyph shared by both scripts; logo size as a page fraction (¼ or ⅙); tone sliders with a marked position; a single home-button chrome on every page.

## 9. UX
The document works well as a portal. A contents page with live links, a home button on every page, and colour-coded download pills make it quick to reach files. As a rulebook it is thin:
- typography has no hierarchy;
- the primary colours carry conflicting RGB/hex values;
- example layouts use lorem ipsum (p26, p29);
- several sections (Social Media, Events) are a single page that hands off to the DAM.

The signature pairing, white on Dubai Blue, fails AA for body text.

## 10. Craft signals
- Exclusion zone = 1 daal (the head of the A), drawn as grey daal glyphs at the four corners (p20).
- Baseline alignment excludes the descending Arabic dots, shown with dotted guides (p20).
- Brandmark width = ¼ (portrait) or ⅙ (landscape) of page width (p24).
- Overlay box opacity of 70% or more, shown with correct and incorrect examples (p26).
- Download pill with asymmetric corners (radius about 24 px on two corners only) that echoes the shape kit.
- 26 unique Bynder deep links plus 65 internal navigation links.
- Tone sliders: four axes, five stops each, and a "THIS IS US" arrow.

## 12. Rating
| Axis | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Aesthetics | 6 | Strong photography and a confident blue. The template is generic: centred running head, uppercase DIN Light titles, mostly empty white pages. |
| Originality | 6 | The bilingual fused wordmark and the daal clear-space unit are genuinely clever. Everything else is standard tourism-board practice. |
| Usability | 7 | Excellent navigation and asset links, clear placement fractions and do/don't pages. Typography and imagery are under-specified. |
| Craft | 5 | Hex and RGB disagree for both primaries, lorem ipsum in examples, white on the core blue at 3.14:1, and no construction drawing for the mark. |
