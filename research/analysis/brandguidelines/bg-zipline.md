# Zipline Brand Guidelines — Zipline Marketing

## 1. Snapshot
- **Subject:** the brand guidelines of Zipline (drone delivery), dated April 2026. `site_meta.json`: requested `brand.flyzipline.com`, final URL is a **Figma prototype** ("BG", starting node 5:16). Because the chapter hotspots could not be triggered, only the cover, the intro/contents, one chapter-opener frame (Brand Positioning) and the closing block were captured (`figma_f01–02`, `figscroll_01–04`, `d00_home_t01`). **This analysis is partial.** Page contents for Logo, Color, Typography, The Airmesh, Voice & Tone, Photography & Video, Layout, Motion and Icons & Illustration were not seen.
- **Why it's remarkable:** a brand built on one asset, the sky. The guidelines open on a clean blue cover, drop to a warm cream ground and use one violet accent, so the whole system is three colours and photography.
- Census (`census_home.json`) only describes the Figma viewer UI (Inter 11/14 px, dark #2c2c2c chrome); it says nothing about Zipline's own type and is ignored here.

## 2. Composition & layout
All frames are 1440×900 viewport captures.
- **Cover/hero band:** 552 px tall (61% of the viewport) with a full-bleed sky photo. Logo top-left at x=95, y≈85–175 (symbol 115 px wide plus wordmark, 435 px total). "WELCOME TO OUR BRAND GUIDELINES" at y≈463 left and "APRIL 2026" right-aligned to x=1345.
- **Margins:** 95 px left, 95 px right (content to x=1345), i.e. a 1250 px measure.
- **Intro text:** about 24 px type with 32 px leading in three short paragraphs on cream.
- **Pill CTA:** "GET STARTED", centred, 185×45, fully rounded, violet with white caps.
- **Contents list:** a 1 px black hairline at y≈430 spans the measure, a small "CONTENT" label, then ten chapter names in ~56 px caps at 78 px pitch, left-aligned.
- **Chapter opener (Brand Positioning):** same 552 px hero band, now a lifestyle photo (family eating watermelon) with the chapter title bottom-left and a sub-contents table (Our belief / category / pillars / audience / behavior) listed at 24 px caps, 38 px pitch, labelled by "CONTENT" at x=95.
- **Navigation:** a 64×64 white square menu button (three violet lines) fixed at top-right, x 1345–1409, and a 30 px violet circle with a chevron "back to top" fixed on the right margin.
- **Closing block:** violet, full-bleed, white 24 px text with the contact line, then "BACK TO TOP".

## 3. Typography
The face could not be named from the screenshots and the Figma viewer exposes no CSS for it. Visually it is a neo-grotesk with open round bowls and a curved-tail "Y" (look at "TYPOGRAPHY", "LAYOUT"), set in regular weight with no bold. Headings and navigation are all caps; paragraph text is sentence case. Sizes estimated from the 1440 px frames: contents 56 px caps; chapter names 24 px caps; paragraphs 24 px / 32 px (1.33); small labels 18–20 px; "APRIL 2026" ~20 px. The logo wordmark is a custom rounded geometric.

## 5. Depth & material
Flat. Photography supplies atmosphere (sky, sun-lit people). The menu button casts a faint blurred shadow once scrolled over cream. No borders except the single hairline.

## 6. Components & patterns
Square icon menu button; round back-to-top; pill CTA; hairline divider; caps-only contents list; chapter hero band; violet footer. The two photo frames suggest a pattern: each chapter opens on a 552 px photographic band with a caps title.

## 7. Motion
Unknown. The brand has a "Motion" chapter and "The Airmesh" chapter but neither was captured. Figma prototype transitions were not inspectable.

## 8. Brand system
**Chapters, in order (from the contents list):** Brand Positioning (Our belief, Our category, Our pillars, Our audience, Our behavior) → Logo → Color → Typography → The Airmesh → Voice & Tone → Photography & Video → Layout → Motion → Icons & Illustration.
- **Logo:** a symbol of two lens-shaped (vesica) leaf forms stacked, plus a lower-case rounded wordmark; used in cream on photo. No clearspace or minimum-size rule was seen.
- **Tone:** invitational ("These are guidelines, not limits, use them as a starting point and apply your best judgment"). The belief is framed on the sky, possibility, movement and human connection, and the closing line says the brand "is always evolving". Contact is a brand mailbox and a "Brand Lab" wiki plus creative request form.
- **Imagery (from two frames):** bright, saturated natural-light photography; sky as a hero; warm, candid people with a sense of family.
- **Token decisions worth stealing:** three-colour system (cream, violet, sky blue) plus black; 95 px margin everywhere; all-caps navigation.

## 9. UX
The Figma prototype is hard to use as a guideline: the chapters were not reachable by the scripted clicks, there is no visible page count or progress, and the menu is a lone square icon. Positively: a clear contents list, a persistent back-to-top, and a helpful closing contact block.

## 10. Craft signals
- Single 95 px left margin across hero, text, contents and footer.
- Cream is used for both logo and ground; the logo is never recoloured.
- A 1 px hairline marks the only structural divider.
- Large caps chapter names create a poster-like index.
- The violet footer echoes the CTA and menu icon colour.
