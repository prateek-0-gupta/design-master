# Olympic Brand Guidelines (September 2023) — Hulse & Durrell

## 1. Snapshot
- **Subject:** The IOC's master brand book. It runs to 136 A4-landscape pages (841.89 × 595.28 pt, rendered at 1400 × 990 px) and covers the rings, a two-tier logo system, a bespoke three-family typeface system, "field of play" graphics, photography, illustration, 460 pictograms and icons, infographics and grids.
- **Why it's remarkable:**
  - The five ring colours become a full graphic language: track lanes, courts and pools abstracted into colour-blocked "field of play" shapes.
  - Every rule is quantified (ring geometry, isolation, minimum sizes in mm and px, tracking units, characters per line, grid margins per format).
  - The document's own layout follows the system it describes.

## 2. Composition & layout
- **Template.**
  - A 1 px black vertical rule at x≈28 px runs the full page height.
  - The left rail (x 42–355 px, ≈25–213 pt) holds the section title in Olympic Headline (≈30 px cap height, uppercase, one to three lines) and body text in Olympic Sans at about 14 px with about 1.35 leading.
  - Content panels run from x 382 to 1372 px: white on a #F3F3F3 ground (62% / 24% on p13), separated by ~14 px gaps.
  - The footer at y≈957 shows folio, a "≡" glyph, the chapter name, "Olympic Brand Guidelines" and "International Olympic Committee".
- **Neutral grounds for colour content.** Graphic-device and application pages use a mid-grey #B4B4B4 panel (sampled) so that white and the five colours all read.
- **Chapter dividers.** Eleven full-bleed colour pages cycle blue, red, yellow, blue, red, green, blue, yellow, green, red, blue. Each has a huge numeral at top-left, the title in Olympic Headline (≈60 px), and a two-column page-number index set at the bottom-left.
- **Cover (p1).** A white top band with the IOC lockup, the title in Olympic Headline, and the date at top-right. Below sits a full-width field-of-play graphic: horizontal lane bands curving into quarter-circle bends, in blue, red, yellow, black, green and white. p2 is a green manifesto page set in Olympic Serif at about 22 px.

## 3. Typography
- **Embedded fonts (pdffonts):**
  - OlympicHeadline: Regular, RegularItalic, Condensed, CondensedItalic, Compressed, plus Regular-SC700 and Headline02.
  - OlympicSans: Regular, RegularItalic, Medium, MediumItalic, Bold.
  - OlympicSerif: Regular, RegularItalic, Medium, MediumItalic, Bold.
  - Stray: TradeIOC4 and FaktPro-Normal, most likely inside placed artwork.
- **Olympic Headline** (p43–44): caps-only, designed 2020 by Julien Hébert (Montréal), inspired by the Tokyo 1964 and Seoul 1988 emblems.
  - Regular for headlines of ≤12 words; Condensed or Compressed for titles of ≤6 words.
  - Leading always "extra tight"; italics only for emphasised words.
  - Also shipped as a variable font with width and slant axes.
  - Fallback: Impact. Non-Latin: Bebas Neue Bold (Cyrillic), Noto Sans CJK and Devanagari Bold, Noto Sans Arabic.
- **Olympic Sans** (p45–46): a 2020 reinterpretation of Akzidenz-Grotesk (1896, "the same year the modern Games were born") by Fabian Harb (Basel). It is the workhorse face; fallback Arial.
- **Olympic Serif** (p47–48): Oldstyle Roman, by Seb McLauchlan (London), for kickers, pull quotes and an editorial tone; fallback Georgia, Noto Naskh Arabic. Each family supports more than 200 Latin-script languages.
- **Micro-typography (p49–52):**
  - OpenType features: case-sensitive forms, small caps, an "(00000)" → rings glyph, fractions, tabular and old-style figures, sub- and superscript.
  - Tracking: 0 for body, at most −15 units for very large headlines, up to +10 for captions.
  - Kerning set to Metrics, not Optical.
  - Leading example: 15/16 pt title, 8/10.5 pt body.
  - Body small size: 9 pt.
  - Line length: 40–70 characters for long text, 30–50 for short text.
  - Hyphenate only words of 8 letters or more.
- **Combining (p54).** Three recipes, Classic, Standard and Dynamic. The Headline section title stays constant; the headline and kicker switch between Serif, Sans and Headline.

## 5. Depth & material
- Flat by doctrine: "Avoid gradients or overt effects" (p82), and no ring effects (p16).
- Depth comes from photography (motion blur on p68, high-contrast action) and from layering in digital, where p134 permits "elevation such as shadows and skrim" for cards.
- The field-of-play graphics fake perspective with lanes bending through quarter-circles.

## 6. Components & patterns
- **Graphic devices (p58–66).**
  - Field of play: six base patterns (track oval, court, pool, velodrome and so on) divided into colour blocks.
  - Cropping: tight square crops rotated in 90° steps only.
  - Sport lines: monochrome stripes and concentric arcs in one Olympic colour.
  - Colour blocks.
  - Combining: field of play + sport lines + colour block, with "a lot of white". Never put two of the same device side by side.
- **Pictograms (p91–112).**
  - 75 summer, 18 winter and 14 demonstration-sport pictograms, plus 95 standard, 187 YOG and 71 Olympic-Museum functional icons.
  - Built on a grid with joints at 45° and 90° and uniform stroke geometry.
  - Minimum size 5 mm / 20 px; clear space ⅛ X.
  - Four container variants (outlined or filled, black or white).
  - They may be cropped out of their container for feature use.
- **Infographics (p113–122):** big Olympic Headline numerals ("1.8M"), monochromatic blue ramps for part-to-whole charts, and single-hue bars. Mixing all five ring colours in one chart is a don't.
- **Illustration (p79–90):** an index of 17 commissioned pieces (flat, geometric to hand-drawn), plus display, spot and supporting types with a cropping guide.

## 7. Motion
Not covered. The only motion hooks are the variable Headline font ("useful for digital/interactive animations", p44) and the lane-bend geometry.

## 8. Brand system
**The rings (chapter 2)**
- **Geometry (p13):** ring diameter = 12 × ring thickness; the gap between two rings = one thickness.
- **Isolation area:** ¼ of a ring's diameter.
- **Minimum size:** 8 mm / 30 px wide. Exceptions only with IOC consent.
- **Versions:** full colour on white is preferred (p11). Monochrome black or white (p12).
- **Backgrounds (p14):** a 3 × 9 matrix. Full-colour rings only on white. Mono rings on any solid palette or medal colour.
- **On photos (p15):** a light, uncluttered area; any black tint in the background must stay below 5%.
- **Misuse (p16), nine tiles:** unauthorised colourways, drop shadows, colour modification, rotation, ring re-ordering or object substitution, overlapping imagery, image fills, gradients, distortion.

**Logo system (chapter 4)**
- **Tier 1** (IOC and entities, set in Olympic Sans, title case):
  - Primary lockup: minimum 8 mm print / 50 px digital.
  - Secondary lockup: minimum 8 mm / 30 px.
  - Isolation: half a ring's height.
  - Variants: French, bilingual and entity lockups (IOC TMS, OBS, Olympic Channel Services, Olympic Foundation for Culture and Heritage).
- **Tier 2** (programmes and events, set in Olympic Headline, e.g. "OLYMPIC AGORA"): horizontal, one-line and vertical lockups.
- **Flexibility (p32–37):** in professional layouts the designation may detach from the rings and lock to grid corners, while still sharing the rings' height.
- **Colourways (p38):** colour, black or white only.
- **Third-party designations (p40):** e.g. "Site of the Olympic Games", "With the support of the IOC".

**Voice**
- Vision: "build a better world through sport".
- Framework (p6): purpose, vision, positioning, and values ("Excellence, respect and friendship").
- Five characteristics (p7): Hopeful, Universal, Inclusive, Vibrant, Progressive.
- The rest of the voice lives in the IOC Style Guide, which the book references.

**Imagery**
- Photography themes: Action, Emotion, Heritage, Editorial.
- Explicit diversity balance across sport, countries, gender and ethnicity (p69).
- Composition, angle and colour rules (p70): no over-saturation, natural white balance.
- Post-processing do's and don'ts (p77–78): no unnatural colouring, no fake ring-recolouring.
- Text-on-photo rules for digital (p75–76).

**Composition (chapter 11)**
- **Print:** A4 portrait or landscape on 12 columns with 7 mm margins and 5.5 mm gutters. "Tight margins are part of the Olympic brand."
- **Social and slides:** Instagram 1080 × 1080 on 4 columns with 70 px margins and gutters; slides 1920 × 1080 on 12 columns with 35 px margins and gutters.
- **Digital:** an 8 px grid with four breakpoints, margins on left and right only.

  | Breakpoint | Width | Columns | Margins | Gutters |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Extra Large | 1600 px | 12 | 104 px | 24 px |
  | Large | 1025 px | 12 | 24 px | 24 px |
  | Medium | 577 px | 6 | 32 px | 24 px |
  | Small | 375 px | 4 | 16 px | 16 px |
- **Ring size** must align with one, two or three text line heights (p127).
- **Fine lines:** 0.3 pt black, or a 50% tint (p128).
- **Digital spacing (p133):** 5–9 items per viewport, citing Miller's "Magical Number Seven".

**Token decisions worth stealing**
- Logo geometry expressed as ratios of one stroke (12× thickness, gap = 1×, isolation = ¼ diameter).
- Minimum sizes stated per lockup in both mm and px.
- Type rules in countable units: word counts per style (≤12, ≤6), tracking limits (−15 / +10), characters per line (40–70).
- An extended ramp of six steps per brand hue, fenced off from large areas.
- A full digital grid table, from 1600 px down to 375 px.

## 9. UX
- A designer can apply this book directly. The contents page gives page numbers, each divider has a chapter index, and every rule is paired with a do/don't tile grid (green ✓ and red ✕ dots in the lower-left of each tile).
- Fallback and non-Latin fonts are named, so the system survives without its bespoke fonts.
- **Gaps:**
  - No motion section.
  - Contrast is never discussed. The pairing grid permits white on yellow, at 1.82:1.
  - Voice is outsourced to a separate style guide.

## 10. Craft signals
- The p13 construction drawing shows the rings on a fine square grid with guide lines at each ring's tangent and centre.
- Chapter dividers use the same numeral and index layout across all eleven, rotating through the five ring colours.
- Tracking guidance uses units, not adjectives (0 / −15 / +10), and kerning mode is specified (Metrics).
- The grid page (p125) annotates every format with size, column count, margins and gutters in its native unit (mm for print, px for screen).
- Pictogram construction shows its geometry: circles at the joints and 45°/90° limbs.
- A colour reference includes Madeira thread and RAL paint codes alongside Pantone and TCX.
- Two stray fonts (TradeIOC4, Fakt Pro) are embedded, harmless and probably inside placed assets.

## 12. Rating
| Axis | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Aesthetics | 9 | The field-of-play graphics, the condensed Headline and the ring-colour dividers are joyful and cohesive. The spec pages are rigorous and calm. |
| Originality | 8 | Turning sports-venue geometry into a modular colour system, plus a bespoke three-family type system with heritage provenance. The pictogram system builds openly on the 1964-onward Games pictogram tradition. |
| Usability | 9 | Every element has quantified rules, fallbacks, a do/don't grid, templates for print, social and web, and an index. It falls short only on contrast guidance and motion. |
| Craft | 9 | Consistent template, construction drawings and unit-level type specs, with colour references down to thread. Minor: stray fonts, and a pairing grid that permits sub-AA text pairs. |
