# Slack — In-house

## 1. Snapshot
- **Subject:** "Slack Brand Guidelines", September 2020 (per the inventory filename). It is a 50-page US-Letter landscape PDF (792×612 pt) in three sections: Defining our brand, Design elements and Governance.
- **Why it's remarkable:** It is one of the most operational public guideline PDFs. It specifies type styles as size/leading pairs with tracking values for both print and web, gives a pre-cleared accessible colour pair matrix, offers a small-use logo with px ranges, sets font-size offsets for localized languages, and closes with a full trademark and legal governance section.

## 2. Composition & layout
- **Interior template:** a page title in Larsseit Bold at about 30 px cap on the 1400 px render (≈17 pt, as 1 pt ≈ 1.77 px here) top-left at x≈62. A full-width 1 px hairline runs at y≈134/1082. Copy sits in a left column about 375 px wide (≈27% of width); the demonstration sits from x≈555 to the right margin at x≈1337.
- **Footer:** a hairline at y≈1017 with three fixed slots: page number, "Slack Brand Guidelines" and the section name.
- **Section openers (p5, p16, p41) and closer (p50):** solid aubergine #4A154B with a white title and a hairline.
- **Interstitials:** full-bleed illustration pages (p4, p7, p9, p12, p23, p30, p36, p42) break the reading rhythm every 3–6 pages. Each has a single flat colour field (pink #F5BAC0 at 80%, terracotta #DE896A at 75%, cream #F4EDE3 at 95%).
- **Manifesto pages (Who we are, Persona, Voice, Brand architecture):** a large Larsseit Light statement in aubergine at about 28 px on the 1400 px render (≈16 pt) with leading of about 1.25, beside a small body column. This is an editorial two-column rhythm.

## 3. Typography
**Embedded (pdffonts)**
- Larsseit: Thin, Light, Regular, Medium, Bold, ExtraBold, plus italics of each.
- SlackCircular: Book, Medium, Bold, Black, plus italics.
- No Hellix is embedded. p18 states the logotype is "set in Hellix Bold", so it exists only as outlines.

**Roles**
- **Larsseit:** headlines. Optical kerning, tracking 0, sentence case only, never all caps or all lowercase. On the web, letter-spacing is **−1px for smaller headlines and −2px for larger ones**.
- **Slack Circular:** all text and paragraphs. Print tracking is **−10**; on the web, **0px for normal paragraphs and −0.2px for hero paragraphs**. Title or sentence case.
- **Slides conversion rule:** when converting Google Slides to Keynote or PowerPoint, use Larsseit for everything.

**Ideal stack (p29), pt size / leading**

| Style | Spec |
|---|---|
| H1 | Larsseit Bold 36/38 |
| H2 | Larsseit Bold 24/28 |
| H3 | Larsseit Bold 18/21 |
| H4 | Larsseit Bold 12/15 |
| P | Circular Book 7/9, −10 tracking |
| Hero P | Circular Book 10/14, −10 |
| CTA | Circular Bold 7/9, **+20** tracking, all caps on an aubergine button (≈105×36 px, square corners) |

The headings step down 36 → 24 → 18 → 12, a ratio of about 1.5 then 1.33 then 1.5. Leading tightens as size grows (1.06 at H1 vs 1.25 at H4).

**Localization (p31)**
- Japanese uses Tazugane for brand, Noto Sans for the app/UI and M PLUS 1p in Google Slides, set **15% smaller** than English.
- Portuguese, Spanish, French and German are set **10% smaller** to absorb 20–30% longer copy.

- p24 shows the core colours as a proportion diagram: White, Black and Aubergine as three tall equal blocks over a row of four short accent blocks. In other words, neutrals plus aubergine dominate and the four accents occupy about a third of the height.
- p25 adds **23 secondary swatches**: berry #7C2852, oxblood #921D21, coral #F2606A, peach #FED4BE, orchid #C05B8C, terracotta #DE8969, pink #F6BBC1, cream #F4EDE4, orange #FFA100, forest #185F34, teal #0096A2, navy #1E328F, butter #FFD57E, olive #779846, aqua #78D7DD, cerulean #2F8AB7 and greys #464145 / #696969 / #EBEAEB. Rules: never for text, only black or white text on them, and "limited" use with core colours. No roles are assigned beyond that.

**WCAG (contrast.py) vs the doc's accessible-pairs page (p26)**
- White on Aubergine: **14.0:1**. White on Black: **16.99:1**. Black on Blue: **8.41:1**. Black on Yellow: 8.88:1. Black on cream #F4EDE4: 14.63:1.
- Blue #36C5F0 on Aubergine: **6.93:1**. That passes AA even at small sizes, yet the doc forbids small blue text on aubergine. Either they used a stricter standard or a different blue in practice. The rule is conservative, not wrong.
- One-colour logo tiles (p21) put white marks on Blue (**2.02:1**), Green (**2.59:1**) and Yellow (**1.91:1**). These are acceptable only as non-text logo use. White on Red is 4.66:1.
- The "Lt Gray 1 / Lt Gray 2 / Text 1 / Active / Null" names on p26 are product UI token names, but **no hex is given for them**. palette.json samples #eaeaea and #d8d6d7.

## 5. Depth & material
- The print system is flat.
- The illustration system brings all the depth: isometric 3D (the cover's octothorpe tile with a hard black extruded shadow, the p7 aubergine city with a server box, the p42 keyhole doors) and flat 2D (diverse hands assembling the octothorpe on p23, globe with avatars on p30, the wave on p2).
- Hard, offset, 100%-black shadows with no blur are the recurring material signature (cover, p9 puzzle, p12 speech bubble).

## 6. Components & patterns
- **Octothorpe (p17):** four colours, two primitives: a "speech bubble" (quarter-round tab) and a "lozenge" (pill). It is constructed on a **19×19 grid**. Both primitives may be extracted as graphic elements.
- **Logo (p18):** horizontal primary and stacked for large scale. Clear space is shown with ghost octothorpes (horizontal) and ghost lozenges (stacked).
- **Small-use logo (p19):**
  - A separate version for widths between **50 and 90 px** (≈0.7–1.25 in).
  - The mark alone at **15–20 px tall** (≈0.2–0.28 in).
  - Horizontal only, never stacked.
- **Backgrounds (p20–21):** full colour only on white, black or aubergine. The one-colour logo goes on photos and core colours, in white or black. On aubergine, prefer full colour.
- **Misuse (p22, 10 cases):** crop, distort, transparency, drop shadows or effects, shuffling octothorpe colours, retyping the logotype, recolouring, outlining the logotype, resizing the mark relative to the type, rotating any part.
- **Icons (p38):** a monoline outline set in three groups (Slack.com, Roles/Events, Miscellaneous) with round caps and a stroke of about 1.5 px at 24 px.
- **Video (p40):**
  - Lower thirds: name in Larsseit Bold, title in Circular Book, background at **80% opacity**.
  - End slate: the Slack logo.
  - Watermark: upper left at **50% opacity**.

## 7. Motion
There is no motion system. The only clue is p40: "animated or still" logo end slates.

## 8. Brand system
**Logo rules**
- **Clearspace:** ≥ one octothorpe around the horizontal logo; ≥ one lozenge length around the stacked logo (p18 drawing, restated in the p44 legal text).
- **Minimum size:** the small-use logo range is 50–90 px wide, with the mark at 15–20 px tall.
- **Architecture (p32–35):**
  - *Primary lockup* (external programs): Slack logo plus entity name in lowercase Hellix Regular with optical kerning and −10 tracking, separated by distance "A".
  - *Secondary lockup* (internal): the octothorpe plus entity name in the same treatment.
  - *Partnership lockup:* a gap equal to one octothorpe width, bisected by a vertical rule. Partner logos sit on the optical baseline of the Slack logotype and should "feel of equal size". The Slack logo goes left or on top. Never pair the bare octothorpe with a partner.

**Voice and tone (p13–15)**
- "Humans, speaking to humans"; conversational but not overly informal.
- Seven "we are (and aren't)" pairs: Confident (never cocky), Witty (but never silly), Conversational (but always appropriate), Intelligent, Friendly (but not ingratiating), Helpful (never overbearing), Clear, concise and human.
- Bans: Slack puns ("Slacker", "Happy Slacking!"), Silicon Valley clichés ("ninjas", "rock stars"), pop-culture leaning, and exclusionary or ableist language.
- Grammar rule (p6): "Slack" is an adjective followed by a description, never a noun, verb, plural or possessive.

**Values (p10):** Empathy, Courtesy, Craftsmanship, Playfulness, Solidarity, Thriving, each with a three-word gloss.

**Imagery (p37, p39)**
- Illustration is "bold, elevated, dimensional", defined by negative space and perspective. A separate Illustration Guidelines document is referenced.
- Photography runs on a spectrum from relatable to aspirational:
  - *Avatars:* warm, quirky, not silly, shot on saturated solid backdrops.
  - *Still life:* simple objects on colour planes.
  - *Lifestyle:* people as part of something bigger.

**Governance (p41–49):** trademark list (SLACK, logo, "Where Work Happens", Slackbot, "Several People Are Typing"), ® usage, screenshot rules, website, trade dress and merchandise restrictions, and termination within 7 days. Disclaimer text must be ≥15 pt at ≥80% opacity, and the trademark attribution line is ≥7 pt.

**Token decisions worth stealing**
- Web letter-spacing in px per role: −2 / −1 for headlines, −0.2 for hero, 0 for body.
- pt size/leading pairs in which leading ratio grows as size falls.
- Localized size offsets (−10% for EU languages, −15% for Japanese).
- An approved fg/bg pair page as the colour API.
- A symbol primitive (lozenge) doubling as the clearspace unit.

## 9. UX
- The TOC with page numbers, a section name in every footer and a consistent left-copy/right-demo layout make it easy to navigate.
- It serves three audiences in one file: internal designers (stack, lockups), partners and press (logo, partnership, governance) and localization teams.
- Weaknesses:
  - 23 secondary colours with no roles invite inconsistency.
  - UI grey tokens are named without values.
  - Governance pages are dense 7 pt legal lists.
  - The type stack's 7/9 body is tiny for slides.

## 10. Craft signals
- The octothorpe is constructed on a 19×19 grid, and its two primitives are reusable graphics.
- Every type style carries size, leading, tracking and kerning mode; web tracking is given separately in px.
- The small-use logo has explicit px and inch ranges (50–90 px wide, 15–20 px tall mark).
- The accessible-pairs page shows each pair at regular and bold, with a stated exception rule.
- Illustration interstitials hit a regular cadence (8 of 50 pages) and each owns a single flat ground colour.
- Every interior page has an identical 1 px header rule at y≈134/1082 and footer rule at y≈1017/1082.
- Video opacities are specified numerically (80% lower-third, 50% watermark).
