# kazam Brand Manual — Good Habit (inventory) / Skep Studio (document credit)

## 1. Snapshot
- **Subject:** a 50-page landscape brand manual for kazam, a London 10-minute grocery delivery start-up (2021). Its conceit: riders are "wizards" and speed is "magic". It covers the logo, a custom typeface, tone of voice, a named pastel palette, gradient "wand waves", and applications from billboards and Instagram to the app, merch and storefronts.
- **Provenance and capture:** the Dropbox download is disabled by its owner, so there is no PDF, no text layer and no `pdffonts` output (`pdf_meta.json` says so; `doc.pdf.part` is an incomplete 197 KB fragment). All 50 pages were captured as screenshots from Dropbox's in-browser viewer at 1201×850 px, which is A4-landscape proportion (1.413). All measurements below are in those px. Typefaces come from the specimen labels, not from embedded font names. Hex and CMYK values are read off the colour pages (p18–19) and cross-checked by sampling swatch pixels.
- **Attribution:** the inventory says "Design by Good Habit". The document itself reads "skep.studio" on p2 and "Created by Skep Studio" in the p50 colophon. Both are recorded here and neither is resolved.
- **Why it's remarkable:** a single metaphor (wizard hat + delivery bag + A→B route) drives every layer: the mark, the colour names (Pocus, Cauldron, Pixie, Bolt, Starlight, Glamour, uh-oh), the graphic names ("wizard whirls", "wand waves"), the promo codes (WIZARDHAT, ABRACADABRA) and the icon set. Few delivery brands commit this hard.

## 2. Composition & layout
- **Shared frame:** every page has two hairline rules at y ≈ 40 px. The short one runs x 51–264 and the long one x 298–1150. Under them sit a two-line identifier ("kazam / Brand Manual", about 13 px) at x 51, the section name at x 298 and a folio at x ≈ 1143. Margins are 51 px on each side (about 4.2%).
- **Content pages:** a small section label sits at y ≈ 207. A 22–24 px headline in Regular with about 33 px line pitch fills the left column (x 51–360). Body copy is about 13 px with about 20 px pitch, set in two narrow columns at x ≈ 353 and x ≈ 628. Figures occupy the right two-thirds (x ≈ 431 or 465 → 1150).
- **Logo and icon pages:** artwork sits on light-grey #EDEDEF boards (p6, p7, p44–46).
- **Chapter dividers:** dividers (p3, p8, p14, p16, p20, p26, p32, p40, p43, p47) put "kazam / Brand Manual" at about 15 px and the chapter name in Bold at about 32 px, starting at x ≈ 611 (page centre-right). Each sits on a flat tint from the palette: Pocus #D1B9F7, Pixie #FFD4FF, Starlight #F9FBCC, Starlight-lite #F8F9E9, or a mesh gradient. Content pages inside a chapter keep that tint: typography pages are #FFECFF, and tone of voice and colour-intro pages are #F8F9E9.
- **Statement page (p9):** Light weight at about 70 px with leading of about 1.0. Selected words are knocked out in white over a Pocus→Bolt mesh.

## 3. Typography
- **Family:** "Magic Mabry" / "Magic Mabry Pro". The p9 footnote says it is based on Mabry Pro, so a Mabry Pro licence covers it. The p50 colophon points to colophon-foundry.org for licensing. Specimens on p10 show Light, Regular and Bold. The text says more weights and italics exist, but none are shown.
- **Character:** a low-contrast geometric grotesk with a wide "a", a squarish "C" and a single-storey alternate "ɑ". It has a quirky flat-tailed "g" with a bulbous alternate (p13). The glyph sets (p11) include a starburst, an arrow, ⌘, a heart and a smiley. p13 swaps the neutral face for the smiley as a "key alternate", so the font carries brand emoji.
- **Numerals (p12):** £4.30 and €5,80 in Bold and Light at about 70 px, which shows the type works for prices.
- **Rules given:** "Kerning tip": headlines −20, Light-weight titles −30. "Line height tip": generous ("likes to breathe"), with no number.
- **Usage seen:** Bold for campaign headlines in lowercase (p15 "ta-da!" at about 230 px glyph height; p28–36). Regular and Light for manual body text.
- **Gaps:** no size scale, no leading values and no hierarchy for digital use. The p12 Bold numeral row prints 0–9 while the Light row prints "01234567890" (an extra 0).

**Errors on the colour pages:**
- HIGHLIGHT and EXTRA print identical CMYK and hex values, although the EXTRA swatch is visibly darker.
- Glamour HIGHLIGHT is printed as "C: x%", an unfilled placeholder.
- "ACAG" appears for WCAG.

**WCAG (contrast.py):**
- Cauldron on Pocus: 12.08:1. On Pixie: 16.31:1. On Starlight: 19.87:1. On #FFECFF: 18.69:1.
- White on Pocus: **1.74:1**. The campaign's signature move, the white word "wizards" on lilac (p28, p29, p35), fails at every size.
- Pocus HIGHLIGHT #A37AF0 on white: 3.18:1, large text only, as the manual claims.
- The EXTRA swatch #8B54ED on white: 4.58:1, passes AA. This supports the note's claim, but only for the sampled colour, not the printed hex.
- uh-oh on white: 3.13:1, which fails for error text. Black on uh-oh: 6.7:1.

## 5. Depth & material
The system is mostly flat. Depth comes from:
- **Wand waves:** soft mesh gradients. "Pixus" (p23) is a horizontal Pixie→Pocus blend. "Cababra/Cadabra" (p24–25) is a multi-stop Bolt/Starlight/Pixie/Pocus mesh.
- **Wizard whirls:** hard-edged concentric bands of Pocus and Pixie, like a hypnotic spiral (p21–22, eight variants: rings, spiral, starburst, zigzag, wobble).
- **Product cut-outs** with "subtle use of drop shadows" (p33).

## 6. Components & patterns
- **Marque:** a single monoline egg/drop shape whose stroke breaks at top-left into a hooked tip (the hat) and has an inner comma-like tick at lower right (the bag's highlight, or the "B" end of the route). p5 explains it with three line drawings: wizard hat, sack, A→B loop.
- **Lockups (p6):** primary (marque stacked over the wordmark), secondary (horizontal), logotype only, and icon/favicon. Only black versions are shown. On environmental walls (p29–31) the marque appears as a ghosted white outline.
- **Boxed CTA tag:** a thin black-outlined rectangle on Starlight or white holding "it's just like magic", "Only → £1.99" or "Use Code → WIZARDHAT" (p28, p33, p36). It works like a sticker.
- **Social:** a 3×3 Instagram grid rhythm that alternates Pocus, Starlight, Pixie and gradient tiles (p36). Story frames are shown on p37.
- **Iconography (p46):** profile, search, trash, location, alarm, payment and basket. All use the marque's stroke weight and borrow its hooked break, so a "spark" gap appears on the magnifier, bin lid and basket handle.
- **App (p44–45):** a gradient splash, gradient-pill primary buttons (HIGHLIGHT→Pixie), and a "ta-da! Your order is delivered!" confirmation with a four-dot progress track.

## 7. Motion
None specified. The whirls and "bursts of power from their magic wands" suggest motion, but no timings are given.

## 8. Brand system
**Logo rules (p4–7).**
- Use the primary lockup "when there is available space" and the secondary when space is tight.
- Never alter or stretch the logo.
- The logotype's height, width and position are set by the "m" in the stacked lockup and by the "z" in the horizontal one (ghost letters drawn on the grid, p7).
- **Exclusion zone:** one "wizard hat" width, meaning the hooked-tip unit of the marque at whatever size is in use. It is shown as a ghosted white marque on the grid.
- **Missing:** no minimum size, no colour-variant matrix (black only, with white implied by p29–31) and no misuse page.

**Voice (p14–15).** The brand is "full of charisma, life, and a sprinkling of stardust". The word kazam is "explosive and instant", and the voice should be too. The closing line is "It's not a rulebook, it's a playground." Starter phrases are set in Bold: abra cadabra / a wizard is never late / try kazam, it's magic / where the magic happens / deliveries in a flash, plus a giant "ta-da!". Lowercase is the default in all applications. There are no don'ts.

**Colour logic.** Every colour has a magic name. Text is "primarily Cauldron". Each secondary hue has a LITE and a HIGHLIGHT tint. "uh-oh" is strictly reserved for error pop-ups. The **90/10 rule** (p23): 90% Pocus to 10% Pixie in primary wand waves. Every secondary wand wave "must contain at least a hint of Pocus" (p25). New waves are made by cropping, scaling and rotating the Cadabra gradient, which is a cheap, generative rule.

**Imagery and products (p33–35).** Partner products (Halo Top, Nutella, Remeo, Fanta, TRIP) appear in three contexts: inside a whirl fading to the background, on a Cadabra wave, or on flat Pocus. Each is "elevated into the brand world" with a soft shadow, and there is no lifestyle photography. Out-of-home mock-ups (hoardings, a station banner, a painted wall, window posters, a billboard) carry most of the art direction.

**Tokens worth stealing:**
- named colour tokens with paired LITE and HIGHLIGHT tints;
- an error colour reserved by name;
- a fixed primary-to-accent ratio (90/10) for gradients;
- a rule that every generated gradient contains the primary;
- clearspace measured in a part of the mark;
- icons built from the marque's stroke and break.

## 9. UX
A designer gets the mood in minutes. Named colours with CMYK and hex, the whirl set and many mock-ups make on-brand campaign work easy to copy. They will struggle with:
- the duplicate EXTRA hex;
- the missing minimum size and misuse rules;
- the lack of a type scale;
- the fact that the signature white-on-lilac highlight is illegible (1.74:1).

The app pages show tone but give no component specs (radii, spacing, states).

## 10. Craft signals
- Clearspace is defined as the "wizard hat" (hooked-tip) width, and the logotype is sized from the "m" and "z" glyphs. Both are drawn as ghost letters on a grid (p7).
- Kerning values are given: −20 for titles, −30 for Light titles (p10).
- Each chapter's page ground matches its divider tint (#FFECFF for type, #F8F9E9 for voice).
- There are 6 icons plus a profile icon, all at the marque's stroke width, with the marque's break motif (p46).
- The ratio rule is 90% Pocus / 10% Pixie (p23).
- The font's glyph set includes brand pictograms (✳, ☺, ♡, →).
- Errors: identical HIGHLIGHT/EXTRA specs, "C: x%", "ACAG", "Cababra" (p24) vs "Cadabra" (p25), "should never to altered" (p7), and Bold numerals missing the trailing 0 (p12).

## 12. Rating
| Axis | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Aesthetics | 8 | Cohesive pastel world. Whirls, mesh waves and a fat lowercase grotesk give a confident, friendly look across OOH, app and merch. |
| Originality | 7 | The wizard metaphor reaches into colour names, promo codes, clearspace units and icons. The pastel-gradient delivery look itself follows a familiar trend. |
| Usability | 6 | Named colours with CMYK and hex plus many mock-ups make it easy to imitate. There is no minimum size, misuse page or type scale, and the hero white-on-lilac pairing fails contrast. |
| Craft | 6 | The glyph-based clearspace and stroke-matched icons are careful. Duplicate HIGHLIGHT/EXTRA specs, a placeholder "x%", name inconsistencies and typos show it was rushed. |
