# New Calendly logo — @mikesmith187

## 1. Snapshot
- **Subject:** A 2.98 s, 960×1280 portrait sizzle reel of a Calendly logo concept. The mark is a "C" inside a squircle ring, shown in about nine hard-cut scenes:
  - navy on an aurora blur;
  - a 3D glossy app icon on a brand collage;
  - a pencil sketch;
  - a translucent watercolour version;
  - white on blur;
  - a blue gradient tile on a colour-block grid;
  - navy on lavender with a wordmark;
  - a construction grid;
  - an enamel pin on crumpled paper.
- **Why it's remarkable:** It proves one simple mark survives every rendering (2D, 3D, sketch, grid, physical pin) within three seconds.

## 2. Composition & layout
- **Mark placement:** The mark is locked dead centre in every scene at the same size, about 180 px across in the 960 px frame (about 19% of the width). The backgrounds change around a fixed focal point, so the eye never has to search.
- **Mark geometry:**
  - The outer squircle ring is about 30 px thick.
  - The inner C stroke is about 30 px, with a counter gap of about 25 px between them, so ring and C have optically equal weight.
  - The C's aperture opens to the right at about 60°.
- **Collage scenes (0.50 s, 2.15 s):** These place a portrait photo, tagline, icon strip, swatch column and "Calendly" wordmark around the mark on an implied 12-column grid with about 40 px margins.

## 3. Typography
- **Wordmark:** "Calendly" in a heavy neo-grotesk (about 90 px at 2.15 s) with tight tracking of about −0.02 em, in dark teal (#0f3a3a) and navy.
- **Tagline:** "Make space for what matters." is set in a transitional serif at about 14 px. Italic sans microcopy reads "Making space for what matters" and "© 2026".
- **Icon labels:** "Scheduling", "Notetaker", "Contacts" in about 6 px sans under rounded line icons.

## 5. Depth & material
- **Gradients:** Heavily blurred photographic gradients carry fine grain (visible noise at 1:1).
- **3D app icon (0.50 s):** soft inflated bevels, a cyan-to-white gradient and an inner rim light.
- **Enamel pin (2.81 s):** a chrome border, white and cyan enamel fills, and a drop shadow on crumpled paper.
- **Watercolour version (1.16 s):** about 40% opacity with a paper texture showing through.

## 6. Components & patterns
- **Logo system reel:** sketch → grid → digital → 3D → physical.
- **Icon family:** hourglass, note, person, card and diamond icons share the mark's rounded-square language and roughly 2 px strokes.
- **Colour swatches:** stepped tint ramps of violet and teal (5 steps each) at 0.50 s.
- **Construction grid (2.48 s):** hairline vertical and horizontal keylines aligned to the ring's tangent points.

## 7. Motion
Measured profile:
- 2.98 s, encoded at 100 fps (high-fps export).
- `motion_fraction` 0.12, but **0 segments** because energy spikes are isolated (p95 energy 46.7 against a mean of 5.7). This is the signature of **hard cuts** rather than tweens.
- `seamless_loop_likely: false` (first_last_diff 26.7).

From the evenly spaced frames, a different scene appears at every sample (about 0.33 s apart), so the cut rate is about 3 per second. The mark never moves between cuts, and its position lock is what makes the rapid montage legible.

## 8. Brand system
This is a concept identity, not a full guideline. Cues:
- The mark: a squircle plus concentric C.
- Primary navy plus a bright-blue gradient.
- A soft "dreamy" photography direction (defocused sky and sunset blur).
- Secondary pale lime and lavender blocks.
- Tint ramps.
- A serif tagline voice ("make space for what matters").
- A rounded icon family.

The system is coherent, but the mark closely resembles a © copyright symbol, which is a real recognisability and legal risk.

## 9. UX
- **As an app icon:** The squircle ring reads at small sizes, and the even stroke and gap should hold to about 24 px.
- **Risks:**
  - The white-on-pastel lockups fail contrast.
  - The © likeness may confuse users in legal footers.

## 10. Craft signals
- The ring stroke, gap and C stroke are near-equal (about 30/25/30 px in frame), giving even colour.
- The outer shape is a superellipse, not a rounded rectangle: corners flow continuously (visible in the grid scene).
- The mark is position- and size-locked across all cuts.
- Grain is applied over the gradient fields to avoid banding.
- The icon set reuses the mark's corner radius.
- The same mark is shown in five materials: flat, 3D, pencil, watercolour, enamel.
