# soft + fluid systems — @ayushsoni_io

## 1. Snapshot
- **Subject:** Four application slides for "Conduit", an AI customer-service-agent company:
  - a frosted case-study card on an orange/violet fluid gradient (1575×1530);
  - an "Operator" AI chat panel with a leads chart over a dotted gradient (2100×2040);
  - an email signature with a blurred orange flame-like blob (1275×1530);
  - an X/Twitter profile mockup with a soft-focus meadow banner (2100×2040).
- **Why it's remarkable:** One "soft and fluid" art direction (warm blurred forms, dot grids, frosted glass) wraps very ordinary UI, giving a B2B AI brand warmth instead of the usual dark-tech look.

## 2. Composition & layout
- **Slide 1:** A centred card of about 655×870 px (radius about 24 px). Inside it:
  - customer logo at top;
  - a scalloped seal of about 400 px holding the Conduit mark (two overlapping circles) in a 270 px circle;
  - a three-line centred statement at about 46 px.
  - Background: concentric hairline contours plus a dot grid (about 20 px pitch) over the blur.
- **Slide 2:**
  - The chat panel is cropped right and bottom, about 1270 px wide, and sits beside a ghosted second panel.
  - The panel header is about 125 px with a 1 px divider.
  - The user bubble is dark (#353535, radius about 40 px), right-aligned.
  - The assistant reply runs full width.
  - The chart card is inset 45 px with radius about 32 px.
- **Slide 3:** The blob fills the top 70%. The signature row sits at the bottom with an avatar of about 100 px, a 1 px vertical divider, name and role, and a CTA on the right. Below that is a row of three contact chips (about 345×70 px each, #f6f6f6, radius about 10 px).
- **Slide 4:** A profile card of about 1465×1235 px (radius about 24 px) on #eceae6. The avatar tile of about 330 px (radius about 40 px) overlaps the banner bottom by about 120 px.

## 3. Typography
- **Statements:**
  - "Scaled without extra staff…" is set in a light, narrow grotesk with high contrast at about 46 px, tracking −0.03 em.
  - The banner tagline "Build customer service agents with empathy" is in an editorial serif at about 48 px, tracking about −0.03 em.
- **UI text:** Inter-like.
  - Panel title "Operator" about 40 px regular.
  - Body about 38 px with leading 1.6, in #353535.
  - Muted labels ("Thought process", "Leads by week", "Save to reports") in #6e6e6e.
  - Numbers set tabular, with percentages in a lighter grey in parentheses.
- **Wordmark:** "conduit" in lowercase geometric sans. The signature name "Ryen Flint" uses the light grotesk at about 40 px.

## 5. Depth & material
- **Frosted card (slide 1):** about 30% white tint, a backdrop blur of about 40 px, a 1–2 px lighter rim and a soft outer shadow.
- **Seal:** two nested translucent layers, a scalloped outer edge (about 12 lobes) and an inner circle.
- **Gradients:** heavily blurred organic shapes with film grain. Dot grids (square dots about 6 px at about 34 px pitch on slide 2) add a digital counterpoint.
- **UI shadows:** minimal. Panels use a 1 px #ececec border and a faint outer glow.

## 6. Components & patterns
- **AI chat panel:**
  - header with icon, title, settings and close;
  - dark user bubble;
  - a collapsible "Thought process >" row;
  - prose answer;
  - embedded chart card: 100% stacked bar (24 px tall, segment gaps of 2 px, round outer caps) plus a legend list with value and percentage, and a footer action "Save to reports" with a "+ Save" dark pill.
- **Email signature:** avatar, divider, name/role, CTA, and contact chips with line icons.
- **Social profile:** banner, avatar tile, Follow ghost pill and tabs with an underline indicator (about 135×6 px, radius 3).

## 7. Motion
Still images, so no motion was observed. The title "fluid" implies the blobs drift. A slow 12–20 s ease-in-out morph of the gradient shapes would fit, with UI static on top.

## 8. Brand system
These are partial brand applications for Conduit. Elements:
- **Logomarks:** a two-circle Venn-like mark (one filled and one outline crescent), and a chevron ">" avatar mark.
- **Lowercase wordmark.**
- **Art direction:** warm fluid gradients, grain, dot grids, soft-focus nature photography.
- **Type:** a light editorial display face plus a neutral UI sans.
- **Colour:** dark-neutral CTAs on a warm off-white ground.
- **Voice:** empathetic and outcome-led ("with empathy", "Scaled without extra staff").

The chevron avatar and the Venn mark feel like two different marks, so the system is not yet unified.

## 9. UX
- **Strengths:**
  - The chat-plus-chart panel is clear: the answer leads with the insight ("Email… 88%") before the data.
  - Legends pair colour with text.
  - The "Thought process" disclosure is a good transparency pattern.
- **Risks:** Low-contrast white text on gradients in marketing assets (slides 1 and 4).

## 10. Craft signals
- Square dot-grid overlays sit on the blurred gradients (34 px pitch), and the dots take on the underlying colour.
- Concentric hairline contours on slide 1 radiate from behind the card.
- The frosted card has a 1–2 px light rim plus inner gradient.
- The legend values are right-aligned with tabular figures; the percentages are lighter grey in parentheses.
- Dark CTAs are consistent across slides (#353535 / #2b2622) with radius about 12 px.
- The presentation ground is a warm #eceae6 rather than neutral grey, matching the warm gradients.

## 11. Reproduction recipe
```css
:root{--apricot:#fab368;--tangerine:#fe7219;--salmon:#ffaf98;--lilac:#ddbde6;--peri:#b6beec;
  --ink:#353535;--ink-cta:#2b2622;--muted:#6e6e6e;--ground:#eceae6;--surface:#fff;--line:#ececec}
.fluid{background:
  radial-gradient(40% 35% at 30% 60%,var(--tangerine),transparent 70%),
  radial-gradient(45% 40% at 70% 40%,var(--apricot),transparent 70%),
  radial-gradient(60% 50% at 80% 10%,var(--lilac),transparent 70%),var(--peri)}
.fluid::after{content:"";position:absolute;inset:0;
  background-image:radial-gradient(circle,rgba(255,255,255,.35) 1.5px,transparent 2px);background-size:17px 17px}
.frost{border-radius:24px;background:rgba(255,255,255,.22);backdrop-filter:blur(40px) saturate(1.2);
  box-shadow:inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(255,255,255,.45),0 20px 40px rgba(120,60,20,.15)}
.panel{background:var(--surface);border:1px solid var(--line);border-radius:24px;font:400 19px/1.6 Inter,sans-serif;color:var(--ink)}
.bubble--user{background:var(--ink);color:#fff;border-radius:20px;padding:16px 22px}
.stack{display:flex;gap:2px;height:12px;border-radius:6px;overflow:hidden}
.legend .pct{color:var(--muted);font-variant-numeric:tabular-nums}
.btn-dark{background:var(--ink-cta);color:#fff;border-radius:12px;padding:10px 14px}
```
Add a 40% black scrim (or set the text in #2b2622) on frosted and photo grounds to pass AA.
