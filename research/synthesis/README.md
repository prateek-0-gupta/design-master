## Top ten insights

1. **Quiet base + exactly one expressive layer.** The best work pairs a restrained structural style (neutral neo-grotesk UI, dark-premium, hairline) with one expressive device (a physical metaphor, a shader object, a pixel grid). Stacking glass + aurora + mesh produced the lowest-rated web entries (24–26 of 40).
2. **Physical metaphors are the highest-scoring expressive layer** (physical-material mean 30.7, skeuomorphic 30.9). They work when the metaphor *does the job*: a stamp prints the date, a field becomes the country, a receipt prints the items. In each case the confirmation is the data.
3. **Motion has a measurable house style.** In 1,368 measured transitions:
   - the median duration is 0.33 s (p25 0.2, p75 0.57);
   - 44% start fast and ease out;
   - entries ease out, exits and gravity ease in, and only machines move linearly;
   - menus and panels open about 1.5–3× slower than they close.
4. **Type is solved; the second voice differentiates.** A neo-grotesk is in 80% of Inspora examples, with Inter the single most-named face. Distinction comes from a mono for metadata and numbers, or a serif for display. Tracking is set per size: about −0.02 to −0.03 em on display, positive on small caps.
5. **Real sites use a gentle type scale.** The median step ratio is 1.167, body is 15–16 px, display is about 3.75× body, and body line-height is about 1.3. Showcase shots exaggerate the display jump.
6. **One accent, tinted neutrals.** The single-accent discipline appears in 104 examples. Top systems replace #000 and #fff with tinted inks and papers (#0b051d, #1e1919, #faf1e5, #f7f5f2) and thread one hue per entity through every layer of a view.
7. **Contrast is the industry's blind spot.**
   - 90% of showcase examples and 86% of brand documents contain sub-AA text.
   - The worst offenders are white on bright brand colours (1.6–1.8:1).
   - Analyst scores barely moved for it: usability 6.64 with contrast failures vs 6.68 without.
   - Compute, don't judge.
8. **Radius systems are small.** The best use 2–3 radii (pill, plus one card radius, plus nested inner = outer − padding), with 0 px reserved for "paper". The commonest values are 9999, 24, 12, 16 and 40 px.
9. **The best brand systems are written as formulas.**
   - Clearspace comes from the mark's own anatomy.
   - Geometry is defined by ratios, e.g. Hulu's radius = shortest edge ÷ 6 and EDP's margins = height ÷ 40.
   - Colour ships as approved pairs with printed ratios.
   - Motion tokens are the most commonly missing chapter.
10. **States are the gap.** Only 4 of 348 examples show empty, error or 404 states, essentially none show focus or disabled, and none demonstrate reduced motion. The happy path is over-designed while the rest of the interface is missing.
